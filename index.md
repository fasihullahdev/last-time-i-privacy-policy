# Privacy Policy for Last Time I…

Last updated: January 2026

Last Time I… respects your privacy. This app is designed to work primarily offline and does not require you to create an account.

## 1. Information We Collect
We do not collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The app stores the following data locally on your device:
- Tasks you create
- Reminder schedules
- App preferences (such as theme settings)

This data never leaves your device.

## 2. Notifications
Last Time I… uses local notifications to remind you about tasks and habits.
Notifications are generated entirely on your device and are not sent to any server.

## 3. Advertising
This app displays advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect certain information such as device identifiers to provide and improve ads, in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Permissions
The app may request:
- Notification permission (to deliver reminders)
- Exact alarm permission (Android only, for precise scheduling)

These permissions are used only for app functionality.

## 5. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share user data.
Third-party services (Google AdMob) handle their own data under their policies.

## 6. Children’s Privacy
This app is not specifically designed for children under 13.
No personal data is knowingly collected from children.

## 7. Changes
This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected on this page.
